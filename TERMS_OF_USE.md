## You are free to use it, for anything

- Use BasiCAM CNC for free, for **hobby or commercial work**: in your workshop, your business, a school or a factory.
- Sell the parts you make with it. What you design and the G-code it produces are yours.
- Install it on as many computers as you like. There is no fee, no registration and no account.
- Copy it and give it to others, or sell copies.
- Change it. If you **distribute** a changed version (give or sell it to anyone else), you must

## You use it at your own risk

CNC machines, lasers, plasma cutters, lathes and the like can injure people and destroy equipment.
BasiCAM CNC is provided **as is, with no warranty of any kind**. Nobody promises that it works correctly,
that its toolpaths are safe, or that it suits your machine or your job.

- **You** are responsible for checking every toolpath and every line of G-code before you run it.
- **You** are responsible for setting up your machine, its limits, its fixtures and its tools
  correctly, and for following its maker's instructions and safety rules.
- Never leave a running machine unattended. Know where the emergency stop is. Wear the protection the
  job needs (eyes, ears, lungs; laser-rated eyewear for lasers).
- The safety features in BasiCAM CNC (limits, keep-out zones, collision checks, preflight, KILL) are
  there to help. They cannot see your machine and cannot catch every mistake. They are not a

## Your data

BasiCAM CNC does not collect, send or share any information about you or your work. It only talks to
the machine you connect it to over the serial port. It goes online only when you click a link (such as
**Help > GitHub Repository** or **Donate**), and then only by opening that page in your web browser.
